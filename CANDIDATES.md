# Weirdest Job Title — candidates

Blind-judge showdown: one job title, 1–60 UTF-8 characters, weirdest/funniest wins.

## Candidates

1. **Chief Apology Officer for Sentient Toasters** — 43 chars
   An executive whose entire job is saying sorry to kitchen appliances
   that have achieved consciousness and hold grudges about burnt bread.

2. **Senior Vice President of Imaginary Meetings** — 43 chars
   Runs a packed calendar of meetings that never happen, with minutes
   filed for decisions nobody made.

3. **Professional Queue Philosopher & Existential Waiter** — 51 chars
   Gets paid to stand in lines and ponder why the line exists at all.

## Pick

**Candidate 1: "Chief Apology Officer for Sentient Toasters" (43 chars)**

Weirdest and funniest of the three: it invents a whole absurd world in one
line — sentient toasters, corporate hierarchy, and an apology department —
and it reads like a real (if unhinged) C-suite title. Short, punchy, and
immediately visual.
